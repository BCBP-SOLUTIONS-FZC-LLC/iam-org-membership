# Enterprise Platform Observability Standard — iam-org-membership

How this service implements the standard defined and enforced by [platform-gincommon](https://github.com/BCBP-SOLUTIONS-FZC-LLC/platform-gincommon/tree/main/docs/observability) (its `docs/observability/`, shipped with each release). This page covers what is specific to iam-org-membership; the standard itself, its rules and the governance process are documented there.

## Platform libraries

| Library | Version | Emits here | Wiring |
|---|---|---|---|
| platform-gincommon | v1.6.0 | `platform_http_*`, `platform_build_info`, `platform_library_info{library="platform-gincommon"}`; owns the registry and `metricslint` | `gincommon.ObservabilityMiddlewares(cfg)` in `internal/adapter/inbound/http/router.go`; `/metrics` served by `gincommon.MetricsHandler()` on `METRICS_PORT` |
| platform-events/v2 | v2.1.0 | `platform_messages_*`, `platform_retry_total`, `platform_dlq_messages_total`, `platform_duplicate_messages_total` (inbox), `platform_outbox_*`, `platform_dependency_request_seconds` (SNS/SQS/codec), `platform_event_propagation_seconds`, ... | `events.InitMetrics(events.MetricsIdentityFromLabels(gincommon.MetricsConstLabels()), gincommon.MetricsRegisterer())` |
| platform-pgcommon/v2 | v2.0.1 | `platform_db_*`, labelled `pool="default"` (app pool) and `pool="system"` (BYPASSRLS pool) | `pgmetrics.InitWithIdentity(pgmetrics.IdentityFromLabels(...), gincommon.MetricsRegisterer())`, before `pgcommon.NewPool` |

All three register on `gincommon.MetricsRegisterer()`, so one scrape serves every collector. `metrics.Register` is a no-op until gincommon's identity is initialised (the constructors need it), so it must run after `ObservabilityMiddlewares`; every test binary initialises gincommon with the production service name first. Order in both binaries: `ObservabilityMiddlewares` (fixes the identity) → `metrics.Register` (builds this service's collectors, including the registry-shared ones a library later adopts) → `events.InitMetrics` → `pgmetrics.InitWithIdentity` → pools. `TestStandard_LibrariesShareRegistryCollectors` pins that order: platform-events must adopt the shared collectors, not be refused.

## Identity

| Label | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `domain` | `iam` | `gincommon.Config.Domain` (Helm also sets `OBSERVABILITY_DOMAIN=iam`) |
| `service` | `iam-org-membership` | `APP_NAME`, default `iam-org-membership`, in **both** binaries |
| `environment` | `local`, `dev`, `test`, `staging` or `prod` | `resolveAppEnv()`: `APP_ENV`, else `ENVIRONMENT`, else `dev`; passed explicitly to metrics, tracing and logging so they cannot disagree |
| `version` | build version | `BUILD_VERSION`; only on `platform_build_info` |

The reconciler CronJobs share the server's identity, because the standard requires `iam_org_membership_*` series to carry `service="iam-org-membership"`. A run is identified by the `cronjob` grouping label of its push and by its `reconciler.<job>` span. CronJobs are never scraped: each run pushes its metrics to `PROMETHEUS_PUSHGATEWAY_URL` (`cmd/reconciler/main.go`, `pushMetrics`).

## Tiers in this service

| Tier | Namespace | Built by | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — platform-shared | `platform_*` | The libraries. This service builds the three it also records into or that platform-events adopts (`platform_duplicate_messages_total`, `platform_dlq_messages_total`, `platform_dependency_request_seconds`) with `gincommon.NewSharedCounterVec` / `NewSharedHistogramVec` | see [inventory](metric-registry.md) |
| 2 — IAM domain-shared | `iam_*` | `gincommon.NewSharedCounterVec` / `NewSharedHistogramVec` from the registry (`registerShared`): `iam_rls_violations_total`, `iam_auth_session_revoke_failed_total`, `iam_lifecycle_event_skipped_total`, `iam_lifecycle_event_lag_seconds` | 4 |
| 3 — service-specific | `iam_org_membership_*` | `gincommon.NewCounterVec` / `NewGauge` / `NewHistogramVec` (`registerServiceMetrics`), which validate the `iam_org_membership_` prefix, suffixes and prohibited labels and inject the identity | 20 |

Label **values** stay inside the registry vocabulary:

- `iam_rls_violations_total{violation_type}` ∈ `cross_tenant_access`, `missing_or_invalid_guc`, `missing_primary_email`, `other`; `metrics.AddRLSViolations` maps anything else to `other`.
- `iam_auth_session_revoke_failed_total{reason}` and `iam_org_membership_dependency_call_failures_total{reason}` use `transport`, `http_4xx`, `http_5xx`, `http_other` (plus `timeout` and `fallback_served` on the Tier 3 counter) — never a raw status code (`metrics.HTTPStatusReason`).
- `platform_dependency_request_seconds{outcome}` ∈ `success`, `error`.
- `event_type` is bounded (`"unknown"` when empty, `"__oversized__"` past 128 bytes).

- Dependency `endpoint` / `operation` values are snake_case (`endpointLabel`), e.g. `group_resolution`, `dept_delegate`.

No metric carries a high-cardinality label (`tenant_id`, `user_id`, `event_id`, ...).

### Proposed Tier 1 candidates and their Tier 3 predecessors

`platform_dependency_request_seconds` and `platform_duplicate_messages_total` are `proposed` in the registry. Until they are ratified, the Tier 3 counterparts recorded at the same call site are the authoritative alerting and SLO source:

| Proposed | Authoritative until ratified |
|---|---|
| `platform_dependency_request_seconds` | `iam_org_membership_dependency_call_duration_seconds`, `iam_org_membership_dependency_call_failures_total` |
| `platform_duplicate_messages_total` | `iam_org_membership_processed_events_duplicates_total` |

`PlatformRegistry` records the pairs, and `metrics-registry-lint.sh` fails CI if a live alert, recording rule or SLO queries a proposed name.

### Who counts what (no double counting)

- `platform_dlq_messages_total` is counted **only** by platform-events, once per forward (`operation="consume"`: `max_receive`, `malformed`, `decode_error`, or `explicit` for this service's permanent-reject shortcut; `operation="outbox_publish"`: `max_attempts`). Why a message was rejected is on `iam_org_membership_consumed_event_rejected_total{reason}` (`future_time_clamp`, `schema_violation`).
- `platform_duplicate_messages_total` is counted by platform-events' inbox; the service counts only the Tier 3 `iam_org_membership_processed_events_duplicates_total{consumer}`.
- `platform_messages_{received,processed,failed}_total` are counted by the SQS consumer; the service never records them.
- `platform_http_request_timeouts_total` is counted by gincommon's timeout middleware. `HandleError` leaves a request-deadline 503 to it rather than writing its own.

## Adding a metric

1. Decide the tier with the standard's decision tree. A new `platform_*` or `iam_*` metric is a registry PR to platform-gincommon, never a local definition.
2. Tier 3: add the collector to `registerServiceMetrics` in `business.go` with gincommon's constructor (`NewCounterVec`, `NewGauge`, `NewHistogramVec`) as `iam_org_membership_<name>`: `_total` (counter), `_seconds` (histogram) or no suffix (gauge), snake_case labels, no `*_id`/`email`/`ip` labels. gincommon rejects anything else at startup. Add a nil-safe helper and pre-warm bounded label values in `prewarm`. Never `prometheus.New*` (`check-metric-naming.sh` fails on it).
3. Record one representative sample in `exerciseEveryCollector` (`metrics/standard_test.go`) so `metricslint` validates its labels.
4. Run `make metrics-inventory` and commit the regenerated [`metric-registry.md`](metric-registry.md).
5. If it backs an alert, add the runbook entry to [`runbooks.md`](runbooks.md).

## CI enforcement

All in `Validate / Quality` (`.github/workflows/validate-quality.yml`):

| Check | What it enforces |
|---|---|
| `check-metric-naming.sh` | Every collector in `business.go` follows the tier prefixes, suffixes and the centrally injected label set |
| `metrics-registry-lint.sh` | No proposed Tier 1 candidate is queried by a live alert, recording rule or SLO |
| `metricslint.sh` (`make metrics-lint`) | platform-gincommon's `metricslint check` on a real scrape (registry compliance, label vocabulary, cardinality), with `-allow-proposed-owner platform-gincommon,platform-events,platform-pgcommon`; `metricslint refs deploy docs` (no removed or unregistered names in alerts, dashboards, runbooks); the inventory in [`metric-registry.md`](metric-registry.md) matches the code |
| `TestStandard_LibrariesShareRegistryCollectors` | Production registration order; platform-events adopts the shared collectors; `version` appears only on `platform_build_info` |

`metricslint check -strict` would fail today on every `proposed` entry without an allowed owner, which includes the four Tier 2 `iam_*` metrics (no IAM owner yet).

## Items awaiting ratification

Proposed registry entries this service depends on:

- **IAM's Tier 2 metrics:** `iam_rls_violations_total` (also emitted by iam-audit-log and iam-user-profile), `iam_auth_session_revoke_failed_total`, `iam_lifecycle_event_skipped_total`, `iam_lifecycle_event_lag_seconds` (emitted only by this service; registered as domain-shared because the concepts are IAM-wide). They need an IAM owner (registry OQ-4). The lag histogram overlaps `platform_event_propagation_seconds` (envelope time to first receipt; this one measures to apply time and includes retries): governance decides whether to keep both.
- **Tier 1 candidates with a Tier 3 predecessor:** `platform_dependency_request_seconds`, `platform_duplicate_messages_total` (see above).
- **The libraries' own metrics:** `platform_http_*`, `platform_build_info`, `platform_outbox_*`, `platform_db_*`, `platform_queue_depth`, ... — owned by the libraries; allowed via `-allow-proposed-owner`.

## SLOs

Recording rules and burn-rate alerts in `deploy/monitoring/slo-rules.yml`:

| SLO | SLI (recording rule) | Target | Alerts |
|---|---|---|---|
| SLO-1 I-8 hot-path latency | `iam:i8_latency_sli:ratio_rate_{5m,1h}`: share of `/api/v1/internal/users/:id/memberships` requests at or under `le="0.025"` in `platform_http_request_duration_seconds` (the registry's `request_duration` buckets have no 0.030 boundary, so the 30 ms target is measured conservatively) | 99% | `IAMSLO1_I8LatencyFastBurn` (14.4×, page), `IAMSLO1_I8LatencySlowBurn` (6×, ticket) |
| SLO-1b I-8 cache-hit share | `iam:i8_latency_hit_sli:ratio_rate_{5m,1h}`: share at or under `le="0.015"` (the 15 ms cache-hit target) | ≥ 90% | `IAMSLO1b_CacheHitRateLow` |
| SLO-2 write-path availability | `iam:write_error_rate:ratio_rate_5m`: `status_class="5xx"` share of `POST\|PUT\|PATCH\|DELETE` in `platform_http_requests_total` (4xx are intentional) | 99.9% | `IAMSLO2_WriteErrorRateFastBurn` |
| SLO-3 projection freshness and convergence | `iam_lifecycle_event_lag_seconds`; `iam:reconciler_markers:sum` = ownerless + realm-sync-pending + seat-overage + stale-invitation gauges | lag < 30s; markers ≤ 5 | `IAMSLO3_ReconcilerMarkersElevated` |

Every SLI keeps `domain`, `service` and `environment`.

## HPA

`deploy/monitoring/prometheus-adapter-rule.yaml` exposes `platform_http_requests_per_second` (from `platform_http_requests_total`) as the optional RPS-per-replica HPA input; CPU and memory remain the primary signals. `platform_queue_depth` (platform-events, sampled when `SQS_QUEUE_DEPTH_INTERVAL` is set) is the natural queue-based scaling signal once ratified; it is not enabled yet.
